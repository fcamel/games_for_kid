⚛️ Atomic Chess Rules

  • Standard chess rules apply
  • When you capture a piece, it EXPLODES! 💥
  • Explosions destroy all pieces in the 8 surrounding squares
  • Kings are destroyed by explosions (no check/checkmate)
  • You cannot move into an explosion zone
  • You cannot capture your own pieces
  • Win by destroying the enemy king or exploding it
  • Explosions can create chain reactions!
