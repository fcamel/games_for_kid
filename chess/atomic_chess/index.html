Crazyhouse Rules

  • Standard chess rules apply
  • When you capture a piece, it goes to your piece bank
  • You can drop captured pieces back onto the board on your turn
  • Pawns cannot be dropped on the 1st or 8th rank
  • Dropped pawns cannot immediately promote
  • Checkmate or stalemate wins the game
  • Click on captured pieces to drop them on the board
